Consent and community rules

Version 12 November 2026

Drinking Buddies helps adults find people nearby to share a drink with. To keep everyone safe and real, we verify who you are and collect some personal information. Please read this before you join. By ticking the boxes on the signup screen, you agree to it.

1. You must be 18 or older

This app is for adults of legal drinking age only. Under Kenyan law, alcohol may not be sold or served to anyone under 18.

  • You confirm that you are at least 18 years old and that the date of birth you give is true.
  • To like, match or chat, you must verify a Kenyan National ID, passport or alien ID. Your age is taken from that check.
  • If the check shows you are under 18, your account is suspended immediately.
  • You may hold only one account. Each phone number, email address and ID can be linked to one account only.

3. Who sees your information

Other users see your profile, never your contact details or exact location.

  • Other verified users see your first name, age, photos, bio, where you're drinking, what you're having and a rounded distance. They never see your phone number, email, ID, date of birth or exact location.
  • Partner bars see only totals, such as how many people checked in. They never see who you are. They can send you deals only if you turn that on in your Profile. It's off by default.
  • Our admins review selfies, reports and bans. Every admin action is logged.

4. Drink responsibly and meet safely

You are responsible for how much you drink and for your own safety when you meet someone.

  • Verification lowers the risk but can't remove it. We can't guarantee anyone's behaviour.
  • Meet in public places, such as the bar on their card. Tell a friend where you're going. Arrange your own way home.
  • Never drive after drinking. Know your limit, and stop if you or the person you're with has had too much.
  • Don't accept a drink you didn't see poured. Keep your drink with you.
  • No one has to accept a drink offer, stay longer than they want, or do anything they don't want to do.
  • If you feel unsafe, leave and call 999 or 112. For gender-based violence, call the free 1195 helpline.

Drinking Buddies doesn't sell or serve alcohol. We aren't responsible for what happens at venues or between users when they meet.

5. Community rules

Be real and respectful. Breaking these rules can get you banned.

You agree not to:

  • Pretend to be someone else, or use photos that aren't of you
  • Harass, threaten, insult or pressure anyone, in chat or in person
  • Send sexual content to someone who hasn't asked for it
  • Use the app to sell anything, including sex, drugs or services, or to scam people
  • Share another user's photos, messages or personal details outside the app
  • Bring anyone under 18 into a meet-up involving alcohol

You can report anyone from their profile or chat. If 3 different people report someone, that person is hidden from the feed until an admin reviews it. Admins can ban accounts, and a banned user is logged out on every device. We may keep the reports and the relevant chat messages for safety investigations.

6. Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have control over your data.

You have the right to:

  • Know what data we hold about you, and get a copy of it
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your account and data at any time, from Profile → Delete account
  • Object to or limit how we use your data, for example by turning off partner deals or notifications
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. Some features need certain data: without location you can't appear nearby, and without ID verification you can't like or chat.
  • Complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) at odpc.go.ke

When you delete your account, your profile, photos and chats are removed. Payment records, the one-way ID code and security logs are kept only as long as the law or fraud prevention requires (see the table in section 2).

If we change this consent in a way that affects your data, we'll tell you in the app and ask you to agree again.
